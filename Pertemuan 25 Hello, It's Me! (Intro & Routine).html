Grammar Core: To Be & Present Simple 📘

1. Using "To Be" for Names & Age

Di bahasa Inggris, untuk menyebutkan Nama dan Umur, kita WAJIB menggunakan To Be (am, is, are). Jangan pernah menggunakan kata "Have" untuk umur!

  • I am (I'm)
  • You, We, They are (You're, We're, They're)
  • He, She, It is (He's, She's, It's)

Contoh Benar:
✅ I am Lina. (Nama)
✅ I am 40 years old. (Umur)
✅ She is my neighbor. (Status/Orang)

Contoh Salah:
❌ I have 40 years. (Salah besar!)
❌ I Lina. (Kurang to be)

2. Daily Routine (Present Simple)

Untuk aktivitas atau rutinitas yang dilakukan setiap hari (seperti pekerjaan rumah tangga), gunakan Verb 1 (Kata Kerja Dasar). JANGAN menambahkan To Be!

Rumus: Subjek + Verb 1 + Objek

Contoh Benar:
✅ I sweep the floor every morning.
✅ They prepare breakfast at 6 AM.
✅ I take a rest in the afternoon.

Contoh Salah:
❌ I am sweep the floor. (Dilarang gabung "am" dengan verb 1!)
❌ I sweeping the floor every morning. (Tidak boleh pakai -ing untuk rutinitas umum).

Vocabulary: Morning Routine 🧹

Ayo hafal kosa kata penting yang akan sangat berguna bagi seorang ibu rumah tangga. Klik kartu untuk melihat artinya!

Do the laundry
Arti: Mencuci baju
"I do the laundry on weekends."
Prepare breakfast
Arti: Menyiapkan sarapan
"I prepare breakfast for my family."
Drop off
Arti: Mengantar (anak ke sekolah)
"I drop the kids off at school."
Pick up
Arti: Menjemput (anak)
"I pick up my kids at 2 PM."
Take a rest
Arti: Istirahat
"Finally, I take a rest at noon."
Wake up
Arti: Bangun tidur
"I wake up very early."

Reading Time: Sarah's Busy Morning 📖

Hello! My name is Sarah. I am 36 years old. I live in Batam with my husband and two children. I am a housewife, so my mornings are very busy.

Every day, I wake up at 5:00 AM. First, I go to the kitchen and prepare breakfast. Usually, I cook fried rice or make sandwiches. At 6:30 AM, my husband goes to the office, and I drop off my kids at their school.

When I go back home, I start cleaning. I sweep the floor, and then I mop the floor so it is very clean. Sometimes, I do the laundry on Wednesdays and Saturdays. Finally, around 11:00 AM, I watch television and take a rest before I pick up the kids.

Comprehension Questions (Open Ended) ✍️

Jawab pertanyaan berdasarkan teks di atas menggunakan kalimat lengkap bahasa Inggris!

1. How old is Sarah?

2. What time does she wake up?

3. What does she do at 6:30 AM?

4. When (kapan) does she do the laundry?

Conversation: Meet The New Neighbor 🗣️

Neighbor: Hello, good morning! Are you the owner of this house?

Lina: Hi, yes I am. My name is Lina. Nice to meet you.

Neighbor: Nice to meet you too, Lina. I am Rita. By the way, you look very young. Do you mind if I ask how old you are?

Lina: Thank you! I am __ years old.

Neighbor: Oh, we are the same age! Do you work in an office?

Lina: No, I am a housewife. Every morning I am very busy. I prepare breakfast and drop off my kids at school. Then, I go home to sweep and mop the floor.

Neighbor: Wow, that is exhausting! You must be tired.

Lina: Yes, but I always take a rest at noon.
